# Problem 1, Question 2: Manure Management With DNR Policy

A dairy farm in Eau Claire produces 1000 tons of waste manure and wants to get
rid of it. The farmer is willing to give the manure away for free.
Equivalently, the dairy supplier bid is 0 dollars per ton.

A corn farmer in Menomonie is willing to pay 0.5 dollars per ton for manure and
can take at most 500 tons. However, a DNR policy imposes a 1.0 dollar per ton
remediation charge for sending manure to Menomonie. Therefore, Menomonie's
effective bid is -0.5 dollars per ton.

A soybean farmer in Black River Falls is willing to pay 1.5 dollars per ton for
manure and can take at most 500 tons.

Transportation from Eau Claire to Menomonie costs 0.1 dollars per ton.
Transportation from Eau Claire to Black River Falls costs 0.2 dollars per ton.
Each transport route has capacity 1000 tons.

The cooperative maximizes total profit. Determine the optimal manure allocation,
the total profit, and explain why manure is diverted away from Menomonie.
