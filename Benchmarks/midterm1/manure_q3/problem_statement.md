# Problem 1, Question 3: Manure Management With Removal Incentive

Continue from the DNR policy setting in Question 2.

A dairy farm in Eau Claire produces 1000 tons of waste manure and wants to get
rid of it. Under the Question 2 DNR policy, the dairy farmer could no longer
get rid of all of the manure. The dairy farmer is now willing to pay anyone who
takes its manure 0.7 dollars per ton.
In the coordinated clearing model, this is a dairy supplier bid of -0.7 dollars
per ton.

Menomonie effectively requests manure at -0.5 dollars per ton because of the
DNR remediation and tipping-fee costs. Menomonie can accept at most 500 tons.

Black River Falls values manure at 1.5 dollars per ton and can accept at most
500 tons.

Transportation from Eau Claire to Menomonie costs 0.1 dollars per ton.
Transportation from Eau Claire to Black River Falls costs 0.2 dollars per ton.
Each transport route has capacity 1000 tons.

The cooperative maximizes total profit. Determine the optimal manure allocation,
the total profit, whether the dairy farmer can now get rid of all 1000 tons,
and verify the node balances.
